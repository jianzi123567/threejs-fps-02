EMBERWORKS — a round-based tactical FPS in three.js. Two teams fight over a foundry yard at dusk: Cinder attacks and plants the charge at Furnace or Crane, Vanguard defends and defuses. First to 5 rounds takes the yard. Single-player against bots, with a buy menu, four weapons, a grenade, money between rounds, headshots, recoil and spread that punish firing on the move. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make the best three.js tactical FPS” bounty, $1,000 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Click to play: W A S D to move, mouse to look, click to fire, R to reload, G to throw a grenade, B to buy, E to defuse the charge, Ctrl to crouch, Space to jump.
